Real Help. Real People. Real Protection.

Whether you're a new user, a longtime creator, or just passing through — you're not alone here. At ECCOOZS, support is more than a help desk. It's a protected space for truth-seekers, creators, and community members to feel seen, heard, and guided.

We're Here to Help With:

  • Account and login issues
  • Soundrooms and session support
  • Subscription and billing questions
  • Reporting violations or unsafe behavior
  • Guardian system setup and controls
  • Creator tools and referral commissions

Who Answers You

A real human support lead — trained in ECCOOZS values reviews your request. For sensitive matters, you will always receive a human response.

AI-assisted triage — routine requests are sorted by AI to get you to the right place faster. No AI closes your ticket — a human always reviews the outcome.

We are building Nia — a purpose-driven AI support assistant. Her name means "purpose" in Swahili. She is coming soon.

Core Admin escalation — serious or sensitive cases, including safety threats, account compromise, or creator abuse, are escalated directly to Core Admins for review.

Silent Support — Coming Soon

Sometimes you don't want to report. You just want to feel safe.

We understand. Not every situation calls for a public flag or a formal complaint. Sometimes you're witnessing something and don't know what to do. Sometimes you just need to be heard without a spotlight on you.

Silent Support will be a private channel where you can:

  • Share what you're experiencing without triggering a formal report
  • Reach out if you're feeling overwhelmed, triggered, or unseen
  • Alert our team to something concerning — quietly, on your terms
  • Seek peace or guidance without judgment

Silent Support is in development. When it launches, it will be accessible directly from your profile menu with no public trace. No pressure. No spotlight.

Special Protection Programs

👨‍👩‍👧 Guardian Program Support

Users under Guardian oversight receive enhanced moderation response. Reports involving teen accounts are reviewed with priority. Guardians can contact support directly regarding their teen's account, time limits, or usage schedule settings.

Contact: support@eccoozs.com | Subject: Guardian

⭐ Creator Integrity Shield — Verified & Certified Members

Verified and Certified creators who believe their content is being systematically attacked, falsely reported, or cloned without permission can submit a Creator Integrity request. Our moderation team will review the pattern of activity and take appropriate action.

This is not a guarantee of outcome — it is a guarantee of review.

Contact: support@eccoozs.com | Subject: Creator Integrity

🔒 Emergency Security Response

In situations involving doxing, stalking, account compromise, or coordinated harassment, contact our security team directly for priority response. Our team will assess the situation and apply appropriate technical and moderation measures.

Contact: security@eccoozs.com | Subject: EMERGENCY
